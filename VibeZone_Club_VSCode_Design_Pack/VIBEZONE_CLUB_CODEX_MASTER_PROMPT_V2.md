## FIXED PRODUCT VISION
**One continuously expandable VibeZone Club**. The player chooses an initial musical identity, opens the club, operates stations, serves & entertains diverse guests, collects earnings, upgrades visible facilities, maintains the Vibe Meter, responds to occasional surprises, and unlocks new rooms/floors and themes. The player never restarts a separate club to change genre. Think nightlife that functions, not a static menu of upgrades.

### GAMEPLAY WEIGHTING (DESIGN TARGETS, NOT MEASURED METRICS)
- ~40% building/decorating/repairing/expanding.
- ~35% operations: customers, staff, bar, DJ, tables, queues, service and visual ambient activity.
- ~25% choices/challenges: Vibe Meter, music mood choices, regulars/VIPs, surprise incidents and optional short rhythm events.
The operation and animation layer must run visibly when player is idle; touching nothing should still feel like a functioning club.
